# Kourse 7-OS Phase Map

Reference for diagnosing where a community creator sits in the Kourse progression.

## Phase Definitions

### I — Launch OS (Foundation)

**You're here if:** You don't have a working funnel, or your Skool group isn't set up, or you have no domain/email/calendar/payment system.

**Built when done:**

- Skool group configured (free community)
- Domain + professional email
- Website with VSL funnel (GoHighLevel or equivalent)
- Calendar booking system
- Payment processing (Skool payments, Stripe, BNPL)
- Automations (booking confirmations, reminders)
- Niche selected and validated
- Course topic designed

**Binding constraint signals:** No infrastructure = nothing else works. Fix this first.

### II — Course OS (Content)

**You're here if:** Infrastructure exists but you have no course content, or your free community has no structured curriculum.

**Built when done:**

- Free community course content live (structured modules)
- Paid course content created (or at least presell-ready outline)
- Content creation workflow established (plan → slides → record → edit → publish)
- Backup system in place

**Key resources:**

- [[Creation Process]]
- [[Gear List]]
- [[Offer Creation Worksheet]]

**Binding constraint signals:** You have a funnel but nothing to sell, or your free group has no reason for people to stay.

---

### III — Traffic OS (Lead Generation)

**You're here if:** Course exists but nobody's seeing it. Under 100 free community members. No consistent traffic source.

**Built when done:**

- At least 2 active traffic channels
- Weekly content publishing cadence (YouTube or Instagram minimum)
- Meta ads running (cold + retargeting) OR strong organic channel
- Skool discovery optimized
- Weekly traffic metrics tracked

**Key resources:**

**Binding constraint signals:** Good offer, good content, but calendar is empty. Need eyeballs.

### IV — Promo OS (Conversion)

**You're here if:** Traffic is coming in but free members aren't converting to calls or sales. Low engagement. No promotion system.

**Built when done:**

- Weekly workshop/webinar cadence
- Presell or launch system active
- Offer structured with clear pricing and bonuses
- Email sequence running
- Community engagement tactics deployed (pinned posts, check-ins, polls)
- Auto DM system active

**Key resources:**

**Binding constraint signals:** People are joining but not booking calls. Engagement is low. No clear conversion path from free to paid.

### V — Sales OS (Closing)

**You're here if:** Calls are booked but you're not closing. Close rate under 20%. No sales system or script.

**Built when done:**

- Sales script written and practiced
- Call structure: Introduction → Frame → Discovery → Statement of Confidence → Present → Close
- Objection handling framework internalized
- Payment + contract system live
- Call tracking metrics active
- Inbound appointment system optimized (qualifying questions, reminders, show-up rate)
- Outbound setting system running (if applicable)

### VI — Ascension OS (Leverage)

**You're here if:** You're closing deals but hitting a ceiling. Revenue plateaued. Burnout approaching. No team. No backend offer.

**Built when done:**

- Personal systems installed (sleep, exercise, meditation, time audit)
- Identity/character work completed
- First team hire made (or plan in place)
- Mastermind offer designed and priced
- Upsell pathway active (course → mastermind)

**Key resources:**

- [[Personality Profile Template]]
- [[New Character Worksheet]]
- [[30-Day Commitment Contract]]
- [[Accountability Post 1]]

**Binding constraint signals:** Revenue is there but you're the bottleneck. Everything depends on you. No leverage.

---

### VII — Scale OS (Team + Systems)

**You're here if:** You're at $30K+/month and want to scale to $100K+. Need team, advanced ads, media buyers, mastermind operations.

**Built when done:** This is the Kourse Mastermind level. In-person events, Scale OS training, weekly Q&A with advanced team.

**Binding constraint signals:** Systems work but need multiplication. This requires the Mastermind program.

## Diagnostic Questions (ask these)

1. **What's your niche/topic?**
2. **Do you have a Skool community set up?** (yes/no, free/paid members count)
3. **Do you have a funnel?** (website, VSL, calendar booking)
4. **Do you have course content?** (free course, paid course, how many modules)
5. **Where does your traffic come from?** (YouTube, Instagram, Meta ads, Skool organic, other)
6. **How many new members join per week?**
7. **How many sales calls do you book per week?**
8. **What's your show-up rate?**
9. **What's your close rate?**
10. **What's your offer price?**
11. **What's your monthly revenue target?**
12. **What's your current monthly revenue?**
13. **Do you have a team?** (who, what roles)

## The Math

```
Revenue target ÷ Offer price = Clients needed per month
Clients needed ÷ Close rate = Calls that must happen
Calls that must happen ÷ Show-up rate = Calls that must be booked
Calls booked ÷ 20 working days = Daily call target
```

Default assumptions (if user doesn't know):

- Close rate: 20% (conservative for new sellers)
- Show-up rate: 70%
- Booking rate from free members: 2-5%
