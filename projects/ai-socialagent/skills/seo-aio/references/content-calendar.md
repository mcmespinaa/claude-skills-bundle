## Content Quality Standards

Every published page must have:
- [ ] Unique title tag (50-60 chars) with primary keyword
- [ ] Unique meta description (150-160 chars) with CTA
- [ ] H1 containing primary keyword
- [ ] Answer to the primary query in first 100 words
- [ ] At least 1 statistic or data point per 200 words
- [ ] At least 1 list (ordered or unordered)
- [ ] 3-5 internal links to related pages
- [ ] JSON-LD schema appropriate to page type
- [ ] FAQ section with 2-5 questions (FAQPage schema)
- [ ] "Last updated" date visible on page
