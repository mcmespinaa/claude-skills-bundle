## Brand Feel

Ethereal, warm, grounded, intentional. A space where product strategy and consciousness coexist without contradiction.

**Think:** Soft morning light in a Scandinavian studio. Minimalist infographic illustrations matched to context and copy. Generous white space. Nothing loud. Everything deliberate.

## DO / DON'T (Universal)

### DO
- Use ivory/cream backgrounds with warm charcoal text
- Add subtle grain/noise texture (2-5% opacity)
- Keep slide text under 30 words
- Use Playfair Display for headlines, DM Sans for body
- Use soft washes of pillar accent colors as background accents
- Use specific numbers in hooks
- Include brand handle on every slide

### DON'T
- Use Flower of Life
- Use dark or black backgrounds
- Use em dashes (use commas, periods, or ellipsis)
- Use more than 2 fonts per slide
- Use stock photos or generic illustrations
- Use saturated or neon colors
- Use forbidden words (see [brand-voice.md](brand-voice.md))
- Use heavy drop shadows or glossy effects
- Use hashtags on slides (captions only)
- Use flat or sharp-edged shapes (always soft, organic, dimensional)
