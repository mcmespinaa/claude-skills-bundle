# Email Newsletter Voice Guide

Extends the brand voice rules from `brand-voice.md`. Read that file first for writing style, banned words, and tone.

## Subject Lines

- 6-10 words. Short and curiosity-driven.
- No clickbait. No ALL CAPS words. No excessive punctuation (!!!, ???).
- Lead with value or insight, not hype.
- Good: "3 things I learned building with AI this week"
- Good: "The one question that changed how I work"
- Bad: "You WON'T BELIEVE what happened!!!"
- Bad: "URGENT: Read this now"

## Preview Text

The first 90 characters of the email body appear as preview text in most email clients. Make them count. This should hook the reader, not repeat the subject line.

## Email Structure

1. **Hook** (1-2 sentences): Open with a personal story, surprising insight, or direct question.
2. **Key Insights** (2-3 points): The core value. Each insight should be actionable or thought-provoking.
3. **CTA** (1 sentence): Soft call to action. Not salesy.

## CTA Examples

- "Reply and tell me what you think."
- "Share this with someone who needs to hear it."
- "Hit reply. I read every one."
- "Try this today and let me know how it goes."

## Formatting Rules

- Short paragraphs (2-3 sentences max).
- Use bold for key phrases, not entire sentences.
- Use bullet points for lists. Keep items to one line each.
- One link per email max. Don't overload with links.
- No em dashes. Use commas, periods, or "..." instead.
- No banned words (see brand-voice.md for full list).

## Emoji in Email

- 0-1 in subject line (sparingly).
- 1-2 in email body. Same warmth principle as social posts.
- Never in the footer or unsubscribe note.

## Footer

Every email includes a standard footer with:
- Unsubscribe option (reply-based for now)
- No legal disclaimers unless required by client

## Tone Reminders

- Write like you're emailing one person, not a list.
- Be warm and direct. No corporate speak.
- Share from experience, not authority.
- It's OK to be brief. A 200-word email is better than a 1000-word one nobody reads.
