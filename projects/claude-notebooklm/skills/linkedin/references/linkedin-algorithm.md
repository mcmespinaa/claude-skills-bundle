# LinkedIn Algorithm & Best Practices (2025-2026)

Research compiled March 2026. Referenced by SKILL.md during LinkedIn post creation.

---

## Algorithm Priorities (2026)

LinkedIn's algorithm now prioritizes:

1. **Expertise and relevance** over recency or viral reach
2. **Comment quality** — the #1 engagement signal. Posts with comments get 2-3x more reach into 2nd/3rd-degree networks
3. **Dwell time** — longer time spent reading your post = more distribution
4. **Contextual relevance** — LinkedIn resurfaces older posts (2-3 weeks) if relevant to a user's professional interests
5. **Authentic engagement** — insightful comments and point-of-view shares beat reactions

## Post Length: The Numbers

- **LinkedIn character limit:** 3,000 characters (~400-500 words)
- **Optimal engagement range:** 1,300-1,900 characters (~200-300 words)
- **Under 500 chars:** Algorithm interprets as low-effort content
- **Over 2,000 chars:** Readers drop off before engaging

### The "See More" Threshold (Critical)

- Desktop: ~140 characters visible before truncation
- Mobile: ~110 characters visible before truncation
- 60-70% of potential readers are lost at this decision point
- **The first 140 characters ARE your hook.** They must compel the click.

---

## The Golden Hour

The first 60-90 minutes after posting determine whether LinkedIn expands your reach. Early engagement (comments, reactions, dwell time) in this window is the primary signal.

---

## What Gets Penalized

- **External links in post body:** ~60% less reach than identical posts without links
- **"Link in first comment":** Also penalized as of early 2026
- **More than 5 hashtags:** Triggers algorithmic penalties
- **AI-sounding phrasing:** Flagged as spam (generic, repetitive structure, overly polished)
- **Engagement bait:** "Like if you agree!" or "Comment YES if..."
- **Overly generic content:** No specificity, no personal angle

## LinkedIn PDF/Document Carousel

PDF carousels are LinkedIn's highest-engagement format. They work differently from IG/FB carousels.

### How They Work
- Upload a single PDF document
- LinkedIn auto-converts each page to a swipeable slide
- Each page = one carousel slide

### Specifications
- Max file size: 100 MB
- Max pages: 300 (platform limit)
- Supported formats: PDF, DOC/DOCX, PPT/PPTX
- Recommended: 1080 x 1350 px per page (4:5 portrait)

### Sweet Spot
- **5-10 slides** for maximum engagement
- Educational and step-by-step content performs best
- Dwell time is high because users swipe through
- Each swipe counts as engagement

## LinkedIn Video Best Practices

- Keep under 90 seconds
- Add captions (most LinkedIn users scroll with sound off)
- Upload directly to LinkedIn (not YouTube/Vimeo links)
- LinkedIn's Video tab and "Videos for You" push native video

## Caption Strategy for LinkedIn

### Structure That Works
1. **Hook line** (first 140 chars) — stop the scroll
2. **Line break** — visual breathing room
3. **Personal story or context** (2-4 short paragraphs)
4. **Line breaks between ideas** — scannable, not a wall of text
5. **Key takeaway** — one clear lesson
6. **Soft CTA** — invite conversation, not clicks

### What Makes LinkedIn Captions Different
- Longer form than other platforms (1,300-1,900 chars optimal)
- Personal stories outperform corporate content
- Line breaks between every idea (readability on mobile)
- 1-2 emoji max (warmth, not decoration)
- No hashtags in the body text. Place 3-5 at the very end, separated by a line break.
- No external links in the body (use comments or omit entirely)

### Hook Formulas That Work on LinkedIn
1. **Vulnerable opener:** "I [personal struggle]. Here's what I learned."
2. **Contrarian take:** "Stop doing [common practice]. Try this instead."
3. **Specific result:** "How I [outcome] in [timeframe] without [expected method]"
4. **Identity call-out:** "This is for every [role] who [specific frustration]"
5. **Lesson learned:** "I spent [time/money] on [thing]. Here's what nobody told me."
6. **Myth breaker:** "Everyone says [belief]. The data says otherwise."

### Engagement Drivers
- **Ask a genuine question** at the end (not engagement bait)
- **Share a specific takeaway** people will save or screenshot
- **Tag relevant people** only when genuinely relevant
- **Reply to every comment** in the first 60-90 minutes (golden hour)

## Metrics That Matter

| Metric | Why It Matters |
|--------|---------------|
| Comments | #1 algorithm signal. Drives 2nd/3rd-degree reach |
| Dwell time | Longer reads = more distribution |
| Saves | High-intent signal. Algorithm weights saves over likes |
| Shares / Reposts | Expands to new networks |
| Click-through on "See More" | Validates your hook worked |
| Profile visits | Indicates curiosity (strong for personal branding) |

## Posting Rhythm

- 2-3 posts per week is the sustainable sweet spot for most professionals
- Consistency matters more than frequency
- Best days: Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday (B2B audiences)
- Best times: 7-8 AM, 12 PM, 5-6 PM in your audience's timezone
- Allow 24+ hours between posts to avoid cannibalizing your own reach
